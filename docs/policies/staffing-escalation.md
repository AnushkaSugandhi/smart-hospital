# Staffing Escalation Policy (VGH-HR-STAFF-04)

Demonstration policy for Vardhaman General Hospital.

## Doctor workload
- Safe load is 10 patients per doctor; 15 patients per doctor is treated as maximum (100% pressure).
- Above 12 patients per doctor for a full shift: call in the on-call doctor and redistribute patients.

## Nursing cover
- Nurse coverage is nurses on duty divided by nurses required for the current census.
- Below 100% coverage: request float-pool nurses.
- Below 90% coverage: escalate to the director of nursing and consider closing beds temporarily.

# Emergency Department Overflow SOP (VGH-OPS-ED-03)

Demonstration SOP for Vardhaman General Hospital. Not clinical guidance.

## Measuring pressure
Emergency pressure is active emergency cases divided by the 20 ED treatment bays.

## Above 85% pressure
1. Open the four overflow treatment bays in the minor injuries area.
2. Start the fast-track stream for low-acuity patients, staffed by one doctor and one nurse.
3. Ask the on-call consultant to review all patients waiting more than two hours.
4. If pressure stays above 95% for two hours, discuss ambulance diversion with the regional control centre.

## Between 70% and 85% pressure
- Alert the triage lead and monitor arrivals hourly.

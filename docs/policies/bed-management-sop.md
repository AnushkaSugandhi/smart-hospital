# Bed Management and Discharge SOP (VGH-OPS-BED-02)

Demonstration SOP for Vardhaman General Hospital. Not clinical guidance.

## Daily routine
- Morning bed huddle at 08:30 reviews expected discharges, admissions and transfers.
- Target: 30% of planned discharges completed before 11:00.

## When bed occupancy is above 85%
1. Open the discharge lounge so discharged patients free their beds earlier.
2. Expedite pharmacy and transport for patients medically fit for discharge.
3. Open surge ward B (10 beds) with approval from the operations director.
4. Review emergency boarders every two hours.

## When bed occupancy is between 70% and 85%
- Review the next day's expected discharges and confirm transport is booked.
- Hold non-urgent internal transfers that would block a bed.

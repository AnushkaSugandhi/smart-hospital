# Oxygen and Blood Stock Policy (VGH-SUP-05)

Demonstration policy for Vardhaman General Hospital.

## Oxygen
- Keep at least 24 hours of oxygen supply. Below 24 hours, place an emergency order.
- Below 12 hours, alert the operations director and arrange cylinder backup.

## Blood bank
- Minimum stock is 10 units per blood group; below 5 units is critical.
- O-negative is the universal donor group; when O- is low, inform the emergency and surgical teams.
- When any group is low, request transfer from the regional blood bank and run a donor appeal.

# ICU Surge Protocol (VGH-OPS-ICU-01)

Demonstration SOP for Vardhaman General Hospital. Not clinical guidance.

## Trigger levels
- Amber: ICU occupancy between 70% and 85%, or a forecast shows ICU reaching 95% within 72 hours.
- Red: ICU occupancy above 85%, or a forecast shows ICU reaching 95% within 24 hours.

## Actions at amber
1. The ICU charge nurse reviews every patient for step-down eligibility twice per shift.
2. The bed manager confirms two step-down beds are reserved in the high-dependency unit.
3. The operations lead checks the forecast on the dashboard every four hours.

## Actions at red
1. Activate step-down transfers for all eligible patients within two hours.
2. Notify partner hospitals of possible transfers and confirm transfer agreements.
3. Postpone elective surgery that is expected to need an ICU bed in the next 48 hours.
4. Record the decision in the Decision Center so the outcome can be reviewed.

## Stand-down
Return to normal operations when ICU occupancy stays below 70% for 24 hours.

# Data Privacy and AI Use Policy (VGH-IG-06)

## What is stored
The decision support system stores only aggregated operational numbers per department per day.
Patient names, phone numbers, email addresses and ID numbers in uploaded files are masked in previews and never saved.

## Access
- Viewer: read dashboards and ask the AI assistant.
- Analyst: also upload data, acknowledge alerts and record decisions.
- Admin: also roll back uploads and view or export the audit log.

## AI assistant
The AI assistant answers from hospital data and these policies, and shows its sources.
It supports decisions and does not replace medical or professional judgment.
Every question asked is recorded in the audit log.
